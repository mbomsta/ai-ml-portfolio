This project explores a clinical decision-support workflow designed to identify diavetic patients at elevated risk of 30-day hospital readmission. The system combines a Random Forest risk prediction mpodel with a multi-agent workflow that provides patient context, retrieves relevant diabetes-care guidelines, identifies potential care gaps, and generates an evidence-grounded clinical summary. This project demonstrates how predictive modeling and agentic AI can work together to provide clinicians with more context around a risk score rather than relying on prediction statistics alone. This is a pilot case study and is not intended to make autnomous medical decisions.
